Schedules

    Copies kept here live in this browser only. Ctrl+S saves to your file, Ctrl+O opens one.

    Safety copies from before updates

      When a program update changes how the calendar is stored, the old data is copied here first, untouched, before it is converted. If anything ever looks wrong after an update, Restore brings that copy back (the current calendar is kept as a copy above first). The last three are kept.

      Week templates

      A template is one pay week. To build one from scratch: + New blank template, Apply it to a week on screen, fill the week in, then Update the template from that week. The usual week is whichever template you last made usual; untouched days everywhere follow it.
